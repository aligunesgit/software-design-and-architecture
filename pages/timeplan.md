The course runs 12 weeks: 10 content weeks (lecture + hands-on lab) and 2 project sprint weeks
(no new lecture, dedicated project work + office hours).

| Week | Module | Topic |
|------|--------|-------|
| 1  | Module 1  | Introduction to Software Design & Architecture |
| 2  | Module 2  | Object-Oriented Design Principles (SOLID) |
| 3  | Module 3  | Design Patterns I (Creational & Structural) |
| 4  | Module 4  | Design Patterns II (Behavioral) |
| 5  | Module 5  | Architectural Styles & Patterns |
| 6  | N/A       | **Project Sprint A** |
| 7  | Module 6  | Domain-Driven Design & Modular Design |
| 8  | Module 7  | Microservices Architecture |
| 9  | Module 8  | Distributed Systems & Resilience Patterns |
| 10 | Module 9  | Architecture Documentation & Decision Making |
| 11 | Module 10 | Quality Attributes, Evaluation & Refactoring |
| 12 | N/A       | **Project Sprint B (final)** |

## Format

Content weeks (1-5, 7-11) run as a short lecture on the week's topic followed by hands-on lab work
applying the material to the individual module's exercises. Project sprint weeks (6, 12) are fully
dedicated to project work: no new lecture, but office hours are available for questions.

Sprint A sits right after the small-scale design block (Modules 1-4), plus an early look at
architectural styles in Module 5, so groups have a working codebase built on solid principles and
at least one deliberate architectural choice before moving into the more specialized
domain-driven, distributed, and documentation modules. Sprint B is the final integration and
submission week.

See [Projects](projects.md) for the group project structure, checklist, and submission
requirements.
